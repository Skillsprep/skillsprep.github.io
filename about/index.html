About SkillPrep

Honest, concept-first study guides for everyday work skills — because understanding beats memorizing.

Why we exist

SkillPrep began as a set of study notes for LinkedIn Skill Assessments. LinkedIn has since retired that feature, so we rebuilt the site around what actually matters: the skills themselves. Excel, Python, SQL, JavaScript, Git and project management are tested in interviews and used at work every day.

Online prep material tends to be either thin marketing copy or answer dumps. We take a different approach: teach the concepts behind the questions, and use practice questions that check your reasoning, not your memory.

What you will find here

  • Study guides that explain core concepts with worked examples
  • Interactive practice questions with an explanation for every answer
  • Full-length practice tests with instant scoring and a per-topic breakdown
  • Links to official documentation so you can check and go deeper

How we write and review

  • Guides are written by the SkillPrep editorial team and checked against official documentation where it exists.
  • Every guide shows when it was published and when it was last reviewed.
  • We don't claim to know what appears on any specific exam, and we don't publish "leaked" questions.
  • Found an error? Email us and we'll fix it promptly.
