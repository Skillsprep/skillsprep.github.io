About SkillPrep

Honest, concept-first study guides for everyday work skills — because understanding beats memorizing.

Why we exist

SkillPrep began as a set of study notes for LinkedIn Skill Assessments. LinkedIn has since retired that feature, so we rebuilt the site around what actually matters: the skills themselves. Excel, Python, SQL, JavaScript, Git and project management are tested in interviews and used at work every day.

Online prep material tends to be either thin marketing copy or answer dumps. We take a different approach: teach the concepts behind the questions, and use practice questions that check your reasoning, not your memory.

What you will find here

  • Study guides that explain core concepts with worked examples
  • Interactive practice questions with an explanation for every answer
  • Full-length practice tests with instant scoring and a per-topic breakdown
  • Links to official documentation so you can check and go deeper

Who publishes SkillPrep

SkillPrep is an independent educational resource. Content is published under the SkillPrep name, and we do not currently name individual authors or reviewers. We do not claim that guides or questions have been written or certified by named industry experts, exam providers or accredited institutions. Treat SkillPrep as a study aid and confirm anything important against the official sources linked from each guide.

Our editorial approach

  • Research. Guides are based on publicly available official documentation, standards and reference material. Where a guide lists them, the sources appear under "Sources & further reading" at the bottom of the guide.
  • Practice questions. Questions are written as independent practice material to check understanding of concepts, and every question has an explanation of the correct answer. We don't copy or publish official or "leaked" exam questions, and we don't claim to know what appears on any specific exam.
  • References. Links to official documentation are there so you can verify a point and go deeper. A link is a reference, not an endorsement of SkillPrep by the publisher of that page.
  • Review. Every guide shows when it was published and when it was last reviewed. We review content from time to time rather than on a fixed schedule, so the date shown is the last time we checked it, and software and exam details can change after that.
  • Corrections. Found an error or outdated detail? Report it via the contact form and we will check it and correct the page where needed.
