Digital Marketing Study Guide: SEO, Funnels & Metrics

Published Aug 8, 2026 Last reviewed Oct 18, 2026 8 min read By the SkillPrep Editorial Team (Expert Practitioners & Curriculum Designers)

Unofficial study resource. Written independently of any exam provider, employer or software vendor.

Digital marketing covers a broad surface: SEO, paid media, email, analytics, and strategy. Interviews and skills tests tend toward definitions applied to realistic campaign scenarios.

SEO essentials

  • On-page SEO — title tags, meta descriptions, headings, internal links, keyword usage
  • Off-page SEO — backlinks and mentions from other sites (a major ranking signal)
  • Technical SEO — site speed, mobile-friendliness, crawlability, sitemaps, structured data
  • CTR = clicks ÷ impressions; Bounce rate = in Google Analytics 4, the percentage of sessions that were not engaged (older GA3 definition was single-page sessions ÷ all sessions).
Common question "What most improves a page's authority?" → quality backlinks. "What helps search engines understand page structure?" → heading tags / semantic HTML / structured data.

Funnel metrics

StageGoalTypical metrics
AwarenessReach new audiencesImpressions, reach, CTR
ConsiderationEngage prospectsTime on site, pages/session, video views
ConversionDrive actionConversion rate, CPA, ROAS
RetentionRepeat businessRetention rate, LTV, churn

Know the formulas: Conversion Rate = Conversions ÷ Visitors, ROAS = Revenue ÷ Ad Spend, CPA = Spend ÷ Conversions.

A/B testing basics

Expect one or two questions on experimentation:

  • Test one variable at a time (subject line, CTA color, headline)
  • Reach statistical significance before deciding — usually 95% confidence
  • Run tests for full business cycles to avoid day-of-week bias
  • Don't stop early when one variant "looks" ahead — that inflates false positives

Practical learning exercises

  • Exercise 1 (SEO Audit): Pick a web page. Write out a new optimized Title tag (under 60 characters) and Meta Description (under 155 characters) targeting a specific long-tail keyword.
  • Exercise 2 (Campaign Math): If you spend $500 on ads, get 10,000 impressions, 200 clicks, and 5 sales, calculate the CTR, CPC, Conversion Rate, and CPA.
  • Exercise 3 (Email Marketing): Draft a promotional email. Ensure it has a compelling subject line, a clear CTA, and complies with CAN-SPAM (e.g., includes an unsubscribe link and physical address).

Practice questions

Question 1 of 20SEO

Which factor most directly increases a website's authority in search?

Why: Backlinks act as "votes of confidence" and remain among the strongest authority signals. Volume of pages or posts without quality doesn't move authority. (Scores such as Moz's Domain Authority are third-party estimates, not Google ranking factors.)
Question 2 of 20Metrics

An ad campaign spent $2,000 and generated $8,000 in attributed revenue. What is the ROAS?

Why: ROAS = Revenue ÷ Ad Spend = 8000 ÷ 2000 = 4. The $6,000 figure is revenue minus spend, not ROAS. Whether 4:1 is "good" depends on your margins — compare ROAS with your break-even ROAS (1 ÷ profit margin).
Question 3 of 20Testing

What is the main reason to avoid stopping an A/B test early?

Why: Early results fluctuate randomly ("peeking problem"). Stopping as soon as one variant leads dramatically increases the chance of declaring a winner that doesn't actually exist.
Question 4 of 20SEO

Which of these is an off-page SEO factor?

Why: Off-page SEO refers to actions taken outside of your own website to impact your rankings, primarily building high-quality backlinks.
Question 5 of 20Metrics

What does Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) measure?

Why: CAC calculates how much a company spends to acquire a single new customer, a vital metric for determining marketing profitability.
Question 6 of 20SEO

Which of these is an 'on-page' SEO factor?

Why: On-page SEO refers to optimizations made directly within the website's HTML and content.
Question 7 of 20Metrics

What does CTR stand for in digital advertising?

Why: CTR is the percentage of people who click an ad after seeing it (Clicks / Impressions).
Question 8 of 20PPC

In Google Ads, what determines your Ad Rank at auction time?

Why: Ad Rank is determined by multiple real-time factors including bid, auction competitiveness, context, format impact, and ad/landing page quality. Quality Score (the 1-10 number) is a diagnostic tool, not a direct auction input. Official Reference
Question 9 of 20Social Media

What is 'organic reach'?

Why: Organic reach is driven by the platform's algorithm and user sharing, not ad spend.
Question 10 of 20Analytics

How is bounce rate defined in Google Analytics 4 (GA4)?

Why: In GA4, bounce rate is the percentage of sessions that were not "engaged sessions." An engaged session lasts longer than 10 seconds, has a key event, or has at least 2 pageviews. (The older UA definition was simply a single-page session without interaction.) Official Reference
Question 11 of 20Email

What is a key requirement of the CAN-SPAM Act?

Why: CAN-SPAM requires a visible unsubscribe link and a physical mailing address.
Question 12 of 20Content

What is a 'buyer persona'?

Why: Personas help marketers tailor content and messaging to specific audience segments.
Question 13 of 20CRO

What is A/B testing?

Why: A/B testing isolates variables to determine what drives higher conversion rates.
Question 14 of 20SEO

Why are backlinks important for SEO?

Why: High-quality backlinks are one of the strongest ranking factors in Google's algorithm.
Question 15 of 20Metrics

If you spend $100 and acquire 5 customers, what is your CPA (Cost Per Acquisition)?

Why: CPA = Total Spend / Total Acquisitions ($100 / 5 = $20).
Question 16 of 20Affiliate

How does affiliate marketing generally work?

Why: Affiliates are paid a performance-based commission for driving sales or leads.
Question 17 of 20Video

What is the primary benefit of adding captions to marketing videos?

Why: Most videos on social media are watched without sound, making captions crucial for engagement.
Question 18 of 20Strategy

What is the 'marketing funnel'?

Why: The funnel maps stages like Awareness, Consideration, and Decision.
Question 19 of 20SEO

What is the purpose of a robots.txt file?

Why: A robots.txt file manages crawler traffic to avoid overloading your site. It is not a mechanism to keep a webpage out of Google. To prevent indexing, you must use noindex tags. Official Reference
Question 20 of 20PPC

What is retargeting (or remarketing)?

Why: Retargeting uses cookies to re-engage users who showed initial interest.

Final tips

  • Memorize the metric formulas — calculation questions are easy marks once you know them cold
  • Know the difference between remarketing (past visitors) and prospecting (new audiences)
  • Review email terms: open rate, CTR, list segmentation, deliverability, CAN-SPAM/GDPR basics
