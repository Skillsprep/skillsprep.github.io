Privacy policy

Short and plain: SkillPrep collects almost nothing.

Last updated Oct 18, 2026

What we collect

SkillPrep has no accounts, no newsletter sign-up and no advertising, and it does not run analytics or set tracking cookies.

What stays on your device

Practice tests remember your best score using your browser's local storage so you can see your progress. This data never leaves your device and you can clear it in your browser settings.

Email you send us

The contact form simply opens your email app. If you email us, we receive your address and message, and we use them only to reply or to fix the issue you raised.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address when you load a page; see GitHub's own privacy statement for details.

External links

Guides link to third-party sites such as official documentation. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Changes

If we ever add analytics, advertising or sign-ups, we will update this page before collecting any personal data.

Questions
