Privacy policy

Short and plain: what SkillPrep stores, and what third parties may collect.

Last updated Nov 28, 2026

What we collect

SkillPrep has no accounts and no newsletter sign-up, and we do not run our own analytics. We do show advertising through Google AdSense and use a few third-party services, all described below.

What stays on your device

SkillPrep itself sets no cookies. It uses your browser's local storage to remember your practice-test scores, progress and bookmarks, your light or dark theme choice, and whether you dismissed the notice at the bottom of the page. This data stays on your device, is not sent to us, and you can clear it in your browser settings. The progress export and import buttons work entirely in your browser.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

SkillPrep shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies, and may collect information such as your IP address, browser details and the pages you visit, to serve and measure ads. Ads may be personalised or non-personalised. We do not receive this data and do not control which ads are shown.

Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: Google requires consent for personalised advertising there, collected through a consent management platform certified by Google. Where consent has not been given, only non-personalised or limited ads can be shown. Where Google's consent message is shown on this site, it lets you accept, reject or manage your choices, and the companies that may receive data are listed by Google in its ad technology partners list. The notice at the bottom of our pages is informational only and is not a consent tool.

You can manage or opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information.

Contact form

When you use the contact form, your name, email address, chosen topic and message are sent to us through Formspree, a third-party form service, which processes them on our behalf (see the Formspree privacy policy). We use this only to reply or to fix the issue you raised.

Fonts

Pages load the Inter typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers and your IP address is shared with Google when a page loads.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address when you load a page; see GitHub's own privacy statement for details.

External links

Guides link to third-party sites such as official documentation. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Changes

If we add analytics, new advertising services or sign-ups, we will update this page before collecting any personal data.

Questions

Use the contact form.
